Case Study · The $47M Business Email Compromise

In 2019, attackers spoofed a supplier's domain (two extra hyphens) and requested an urgent wire change. No second-channel verification was performed. The loss was $47M.

  • Sender domain had a subtle lookalike prefix.
  • Urgency ("today", "before close of business").
  • Changed bank details in a PDF attachment.
  • No call-back verification against a known number.
